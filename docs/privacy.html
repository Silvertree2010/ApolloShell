Privacy Policy

Last updated 21 September 2026

ApolloShell has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking. The app does not send anything about you or your Mac to me.

The app

Your settings, pinned apps, layout and themes are stored only on your Mac, under ~/Library/Application Support/ApolloShell. System information such as battery, Bluetooth, network, CPU and the song that is playing is read on your Mac and never leaves it.

The app makes these network requests:

  • Weather. The coordinates of the place you chose go to the weather service you picked: Open-Meteo, MET Norway or wttr.in. ApolloShell does not use your Mac's location.
  • Place search. When you search for a place, the text you type goes to the Open-Meteo geocoding service.
  • Updates. The app asks GitHub whether a new version exists.

These services see your IP address, as any website would. Their own privacy policies apply.

The website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log your IP address for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement. To show the star count, your browser asks the GitHub API once per session and keeps the number in session storage. The site sets no cookies and loads no fonts or scripts from other servers.
